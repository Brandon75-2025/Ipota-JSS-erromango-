Ipota Junior Secondary School Located in Southeast Erromango, Tafea Province, we provide accessible, quality secondary education to students from villages across the island. With on-campus boarding facilities, dedicated classrooms, and hands-on community programs, we empower Erromango's youth with the knowledge and skills to thrive.

  • Location: Ipota, East Erromango, Vanuatu
  • Facilities: Classrooms, Dining Kitchen, Boys' & Girls' Dormitories
  • Programs: Junior Secondary Curriculum & Youth Agriculture Initiatives
